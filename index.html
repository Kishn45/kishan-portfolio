01 — About

A little about me.

Kishan Kumar is an Electronics & Communication Engineering student at St. Joseph Engineering College, Mangalore — and, alongside that, a founder running two ventures already in motion. Returno is his digital growth and branding studio, built to help businesses tell better stories and grow faster. GreenSakhi is his sustainability initiative, focused on community-driven environmental impact.

Between coursework and client work, he's spent the last couple of years teaching himself how to think in products — turning ideas into PRDs, prioritization frameworks and working MVPs, then testing them against real users. The thread running through all of it is engineering discipline applied to business problems: understand the system, define what matters, build, measure, improve.
